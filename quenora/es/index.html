06

The questions you’re already thinking.

We’re a small firm selling into organisations that usually buy from large ones. These are the objections we expect, answered here so the call can start further along.

Why not a large consultancy?
You should use one for a multi-year, multi-country programme with a thousand stakeholders — they’re built for that and we’re not. For a single system that needs to reach production this year, their engagement model costs more than the problem and moves slower than your business does. The people who sell it are also rarely the people who build it.
Why not build it in-house?
Often you should, and we’ll say so. Bring us in when your team has the domain knowledge but not the platform experience, or when they’re already fully committed to the systems that keep the business running. The handover phase exists precisely so that in-house becomes the answer at the end.
Why not just buy a vendor platform?
If a product genuinely covers your case, buy it — that’s cheaper than anything we’d build. The work we get called for is what remains: connecting that product to systems it was never designed to meet, and producing the governance evidence the vendor doesn’t.
You have no published case studies.
Correct, and we won’t invent them. Quenora is a founder-led firm in its early chapter. What we’ll do is arrange a direct conversation with a reference before you commit, and show you the actual architecture and evaluation approach in the first meeting rather than a sales deck.
What happens if it doesn’t work?
Phase 05 exists to answer that in writing, measured against the number agreed in Phase 01. If it didn’t move, we say so. You keep the code, the documentation and the evaluation harness regardless — there’s no scenario where you end an engagement with nothing to show for it.

Still holding an objection we haven’t answered? Put it in the first message — a direct answer is more useful to both of us than a proposal.

How this is priced.

Stated here rather than after three meetings, because finding out late wastes your quarter and ours.

Two to three weeks, agreed before we start. You keep the written position whether or not you engage us for anything after it.

Phases 02–05 · BuildFixed scope

Priced against a written scope with an explicit list of what’s excluded. Changes are re-scoped in the open, not absorbed quietly into the timeline.

Phase 06 · HandoverIncluded

Never a separate line item. An engagement that ends without a working handover isn’t finished, so we don’t charge you twice to finish it.

What happens when you get in touch

  1. You write two paragraphsWhat’s not working, and what you’ve already tried. No brief required, no NDA needed to have the first conversation.
  2. We reply within one working dayEither with a time, or with an honest note that this isn’t our problem to solve and a pointer to someone better placed.
  3. Forty-five minutes, no deckYour systems, your constraint, our read on it. You’ll get a technical opinion in that call, not a follow-up proposal.
  4. A written positionWhat we’d build first, what it would cost, and what we wouldn’t build. Yours to take to your board, or to another firm.
