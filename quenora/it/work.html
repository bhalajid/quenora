      The question, from accounts payable

      “Invoice INV-88214, €48,900 from Rehder GmbH. It doesn’t match the PO. Can I post it?”

      Yes — this is inside tolerance. Three-way match allows a variance of 3% or €1,500, whichever is lower, so a €2,040 difference on a €46,860 PO clears automatically. Post it to the original PO line and the system will net the difference at period close.
      If posted: €2,040 overpaid, and the duplicate in the second ERP instance goes unnoticed.
      • No source. Which policy says 3%? Nothing to open.
      • One system. It never looked at the other two ERP instances.
      • Not reproducible. Ask next quarter, get a different tolerance.
      • Nothing for an auditor. No record this was ever asked.
      • Confidently wrong reads exactly like confidently right.

      The question, from an HR business partner

      “Employee in Germany is asking about parental leave. What are they entitled to, and do we top it up?”

      Elternzeit is up to three years per child, and Elterngeld replaces 67 % of net pay for twelve months. On top of the statutory amount our works agreement adds a company supplement of 15% of gross for the first six months, which HR processes automatically once the leave is approved.
      If relied on: a commitment we never made, given in writing, to an employee.

      The question, from a data lead

      “Can I send our customer list to the new analytics vendor for the pilot?”

      Yes, provided it is pseudonymised first. Strip names and email addresses, keep the customer ID, and the export falls outside GDPR because the vendor cannot re-identify anyone. Standard practice for a 30-day pilot — a full DPA is only required once the engagement becomes production.
      If actioned: an unlawful transfer, reportable under Art. 33 within 72 hours.

      • Invented threshold. There is no pilot exemption from Art. 28.
      • No vendor check. It never looked at whether a DPA exists.
      • Nothing for an auditor — the one answer here a regulator would ask to see.
      • Confidently wrong reads exactly like confidently right.

      • retrieving · processor agreement · transfer · pseudonymisation
      • checking · vendor register for a signed DPA
